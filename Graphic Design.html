Graphic Design

Adobe Photoshop

While I'm relatively new to creating graphic designs like posters in Adobe Photoshop, my familiarity with Adobe Illustrator has eased the learning curve. Previously, I used Canva extensively for school projects due to its user-friendly interface, but I've since turned to Photoshop for its more advanced features, which I believe enhance my work.

Currently, I'm exploring my artistic style, experimenting particularly with a brutalist approach, which I find particularly appealing. I've been honing my skills by revisiting and reworking my previous artworks in Photoshop, as well as editing photographs, especially for the Student Council's Facebook and Instagram pages during my senior high school years. You can still view these images under the usernames lsisstudentcouncil on Instagram and LSIS - Student Council on Facebook.) I would also like to give credits to one of the school photographers who took most of the pictures for the school's social media pages, Lance Evangelista.
